Before you ask.

I haven't trained in years. Is that okay?

That's most people on day one. Your intro session sets weights for where you are now, not where you used to be.

Do I need to book classes?

Yes, in the app. Classes cap at 12, so booking means a coach can actually watch your lifts.

What happens after the free week?

Nothing automatic. We'll text you on day seven. If you want to keep going, reply and we'll set up a plan. If not, that's it.

Is there parking?

Free street parking after 6pm, and bike racks out front.
