Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

I'm Maribeth Bañas, and I run Maribloom, a freelance automation service for salons, spas and gyms. This page explains what information I collect when you contact me, why I need it, and how you stay in control of it.

01What I collect

When you fill in my inquiry form or book a call, I collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name and website or social media link
  • Your form answers: your type of business, your booking challenges, how clients book with you, and any extra details you choose to share, such as your timeline or notes

I don't ask for payment details, ID numbers or anything you haven't chosen to give me.

02Why I use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and send you my booking link
  • To prepare for our discovery call, so I already understand your business
  • To send automation tips, service updates and follow-up emails, only if you opt in

I use your details to answer the request you made, and for marketing emails only with your consent. You can change your mind at any time.

03Tools that handle your data

I use a small set of trusted tools to run my business:

  • Tally: my inquiry form
  • Calendly: booking calls
  • Google Sheets and Gmail: storing inquiries and sending emails
  • n8n: automating replies and follow-ups
  • An AI model: helping me summarise and sort inquiries, so I can reply faster

Some of these tools store data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. I only use services that protect personal data with industry-standard security.

I never sell, rent or trade your information.

04How long I keep it

I keep your information until you ask me to delete it, or for 24 months after we were last in contact, whichever comes first. After that, I delete it.

05Your rights

  • See the information I have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time, using the link at the bottom of every email or by emailing me

To make any request, email me at workwithmaribloom@gmail.com. I'll reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with how I handle your data, you can also contact your local data protection authority, such as the ICO in the UK or the OAIC in Australia.

06Cookies

This site only uses the cookies and similar technology needed for the site and its embedded tools (like my Tally form) to work. I don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

07Contact

Questions about this policy? Email me at workwithmaribloom@gmail.com.

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
