Questions first-timers ask.

What should I wear?

Whatever's comfortable. We'll give you a robe, and you undress only to your comfort level. You're always covered except for the area being worked on.

Can I choose a male or female therapist?

Yes. Add it in the notes when we text to confirm, and we'll match you.

What if I need to cancel?

Change or cancel free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge half the treatment price.

Do you have gift cards?

Yes. They're sent by email in a few minutes and never expire.
