The Story

Fifteen years of making sure nothing gets missed.

For more than fifteen years I've run busy households in Malaysia, Singapore and Dubai: the schedules, the reminders, the small follow-ups that keep everything moving without anyone noticing. Now I build that same quiet reliability for salons, spas and gyms. My automations answer every missed call, ask happy clients for reviews, and bring regulars back before they drift away.

  • A missed call is a lost booking

    When nobody picks up, people book with whoever does. The first reply should go out on its own, while they still want the appointment.

  • Regulars are the business

    Bringing back a client who already likes you is easier than finding a new one. Win-back comes before ads.

  • Built for a busy front desk

    If your team can't run it on a packed Saturday, it doesn't work. I keep every system simple enough to run without me.

  • Proof over promises

    I don't quote results I can't show. I show you exactly what I built, how it works and what it costs.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I have to change my booking system?

Usually not. I work with the tools you already use wherever possible. On the free call we look at your setup and decide what fits.

Do I need GoHighLevel?

Not always. For text-back, review requests and win-back, GoHighLevel keeps everything in one place. If you already use another system, we check what works with it first.

How much does it cost?

Every package has one fixed price, agreed before any work starts. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the package and your current tools. The timeline is agreed upfront in your plan, so you know the go-live date before we start.

Will my team know how to use it?

Yes. You get a recorded walkthrough of everything I built, so your front desk can run it without me.

Where are your clients based?

I work remotely with salons, spas and gyms in the US, UK and Australia, and plan calls around your business hours.
