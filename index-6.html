    Before you press start

    Common questions.

    How much does it cost?

    Every package has one fixed price, agreed before any work starts. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices. Not included: your tool subscriptions, SMS fees or ads.

    Will it work with my booking system?

    Often, yes, but I check first. On the free call we look at your booking system, phone and email tools, and whether they allow outside connections. If yours is closed, I suggest a plan B before I quote a price, so there are no surprises.

    Do I need GoHighLevel?

    Not always. GoHighLevel keeps text-back, review requests and win-back in one place. If you already use another system, we check what works with it first.

    How long does setup take?

    It depends on the package and your current tools. The timeline is agreed upfront in your plan, so you know the go-live date before we start.

    Will my team know how to use it?

    Yes. You get a recorded walkthrough of everything I built, so your front desk can run it without me.

    Is it okay to text my clients like this?

    When the system follows the messaging rules that apply to you. Before launch I check how permission was collected and set up consent checks and opt-outs, so promotional messages only go out when permitted. Rules vary by country and message type.

    What happens after handover?

    Everything is set up in your own accounts, so you own it. For 30 days I fix anything that doesn't work as agreed and make small tweaks to wording or timing. After that, ongoing care is an optional monthly plan.

    Where are your clients based?

    I work remotely with salons, spas and gyms in the US, UK and Australia, and schedule calls at times that work for both of us.

    How this is calculated

    The starting numbers are examples only. Change them to match your business. This is a rough estimate, not a guarantee.

    Lost bookings = missed calls per month × % who wanted to book × % who never called back. Lost revenue = lost bookings × average spend. Won back = lost revenue × the % you think a text-back could recover.

    Each lost booking is counted once. Repeat visits and referrals aren't included.
