Problem
Leads from the US, UK and Australia want to book a call at any hour, but I can only take calls at set times in Dubai. Answering and scheduling by hand is slow, and leads go cold.
What I built
  • Booking form → the system checks my call hours and my calendar.

  • An AI step reads the lead's notes, writes a personal email, and flags vague or not-a-fit leads.
  • Retries plus an error alert if anything fails.

Services

Automation for salons, spas and gyms that run on regulars.

Three core services help you respond to missed inquiries, collect honest reviews and follow up with regulars.

Bookings

Missed-Call Text-Back

When your front desk can't pick up, your phone and messaging setup can send the caller a friendly text with your booking link.

Example messageAfter a missed call
Hi! Sorry we missed your call at Stillwater. Want to book in? Pick a time here: [booking link]
  • Prompt follow-up after a missed call
  • Your booking link in every message
  • Tested before handover
Reviews

Automatic Review Requests

After completed visits, eligible clients receive a friendly, automated request for an honest Google review, with an optional gentle reminder. Every customer has an equal opportunity to share their experience.

Example messageAfter the visit
Thanks for coming in today, Maya! If you have a minute, we'd love an honest review: [review link]
  • Sent after completed visits
  • Optional gentle reminder
  • Every client gets the same chance to review
Regulars

Client Win-Back Campaign

With appropriate marketing permission, clients who haven't booked in 60 or 90 days can receive a friendly invitation to return. I organize contacts by their last visit.

Example message60 days later
Hi Jess, it's been a while! Your favourite slot is open this Saturday. Want it? [booking link]
  • Contacts sorted by last visit
  • Friendly invite at 60 or 90 days
  • Only to clients who gave marketing permission

Before you ask

Do I have to change my booking system?

Usually not. I work with the tools you already use wherever possible. On the free call we look at your setup and decide what fits.

Do I need GoHighLevel?

Not always. For text-back, review requests and win-back, GoHighLevel keeps everything in one place. If you already use another system, we check what works with it first.

How much does it cost?

Every package has one fixed price, agreed before any work starts. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

How long does setup take?

It depends on the package and your current tools. The timeline is agreed upfront in your plan, so you know the go-live date before we start.

Will my team know how to use it?

Yes. You get a recorded walkthrough of everything I built, so your front desk can run it without me.

Is it okay to text my clients like this?

Yes, when the system follows applicable messaging rules. Before launching, I check how customer permission was collected, configure consent checks and opt-outs, and help ensure promotional messages are sent only when permitted. Requirements vary by country and message type.

What happens after handover?

Everything is set up in your own accounts, so you own it and pay your tools directly. For 30 days after handover, I fix anything that doesn't work as agreed, watch for errors and make small adjustments like text wording or timing. New features or extra steps are quoted separately. After that, ongoing care is optional as a monthly plan.

Where are your clients based?

I work remotely with salons, spas and gyms in the US, UK and Australia, and schedule project calls at times that work for both of us.

How this is calculated

The starting numbers are examples only. Change them to match your business. This is a rough estimate, not a guarantee.

Lost bookings = missed calls per month × % who wanted to book × % who never called back.

Lost revenue = lost bookings × average spend.

Won back = lost revenue × the % you think a text-back could recover.

Monthly estimates use 52 weeks divided by 12. They model gross booking value before costs and exclude repeat visits and referrals.
